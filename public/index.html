How this report is calculated

Wastage includes W21, W22, W20, W24, W25 and B02. Issued quantity includes every transaction with trans_type = I, regardless of work center. Both use negative SUM(qty): stock outflows appear positive and inflows offset them. A transaction matching both rules contributes to both measures.

Monthly % = monthly wastage ÷ monthly issued × 100. Cumulative % = running wastage ÷ running issued × 100, starting at the selected From month. A zero or negative denominator displays N/A. Empty months show zero quantities. Totals are weighted ratios, never averages of percentages.

Categories use FLVGroup from BTE_LOTAging only. Missing groups appear as Unclassified. Descriptions are joined once per item from BTE_LOTAging; missing descriptions are marked. Cross-item quantities may mix units; filter to comparable materials before interpreting combined quantities or rates.
